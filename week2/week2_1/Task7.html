Korean Kimpab Recipe

What You Need

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • sausage
  • fried egg
  • Sesame oil
  • cucumber

Steps

  1. Cook the rice and let it cool slightly.
  2. Warning: use medium heat when frying the egg.
  3. Cut and season the vegetables separately.
  4. Place rice in a bowl; arrange vegetables and egg on top.
  5. Add gochujang and sesame oil, then roll everything together.
